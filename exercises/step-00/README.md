# Step 0: explore the baseline

Open [the app](http://localhost:8000) after completing the repository setup.
Use your chosen port if it differs from 8000.

## Explore the map

Zoom into the Le Porge wildfire perimeter. The portfolio contains 100 synthetic
sites belonging to 15 companies. Click a site to see its company and insured value.
Use the legend to identify site locations, buildings and parcels; building and
parcel shapes appear as you zoom in. Try the street and satellite basemaps.
Imagery provides geographic context; it is not evidence of wildfire damage.

A site qualifies when any building intersects the perimeter, including boundary
contact. A location pin outside the perimeter does not exclude that site. Count
each site and its full building and contents TIV once. Exposure is not loss.

## Ask and save

Ask these three questions:

1. Which sites lie inside the wildfire footprint, and how many distinct sites is that?
2. What TIV do these sites represent, and which companies have the highest exposed TIV?
3. For each company, what share of its full portfolio TIV is inside the footprint?

Save the answers and any highlighted selection with a screenshot or a short note
before restarting or refreshing: refreshing starts a fresh chat and map.
Open **Example prompts** beside the chat input to find the questions again.

The starter assistant can read data and control the map. It has no registered
calculation tools. It may produce an answer, approximate, or decline. Look at the
map: are selected buildings inside or touching the perimeter? Are qualifying sites
missing? Record what needs checking, including a refusal or an error. Do not assume
that a plausible number or a highlighted map establishes a correct calculation.
